# Preparing the Lab, and Putting It Away

The table and the platforms must be prepared before every session: dust on the granite or on the air bearings can scratch them, and the bearings are expensive. Wear gloves whenever you touch the bearings' faces or the table top.

## Before a session

### 1. Uncover the table

1. With two people if you can, one on each side, fold the cover off the table. Wear gloves, and a lab coat if your clothes could scratch the surface.
2. Put the folded cover on a chair or another clean surface, **never on the floor**.

### 2. Clean the table

1. Lower the table's barriers, so dust can be wiped off the edges.
2. Wipe the granite gently with the blue shop towels, pushing debris towards the edge; don't press particles into the surface.
3. For a deeper clean, spray isopropyl alcohol on the table and wipe it with a shop towel. Read the alcohol's safety sheet first, and wear gloves and eye protection. The alcohol is kept in the yellow *Flammable Items* cabinet outside the lab.
4. Put the barriers back up and secure each corner with the long bolts from the *Table* drawer.

### 3. Prepare each platform

The platforms must be assembled, with charged batteries and a full air tank ([The Air Compressor](page:lab-air)).

1. **Install the air tank.** Remove the tank's cap, line the tank up with its fitting on the lowest level, and turn it clockwise to attach it (you may need to take off the acrylic face cover). Turn the valve clockwise to let air into the platform: both pressure gauges should rise.
2. **Clean the air bearings.** With gloves, put the platform on its stand so the bearings are clear of everything. Power the platform on (its power panel's light comes on). On the lab console's [Hardware tab](page:tab-hardware), switch the platform's **Pucks** on: you hear a click and the bearing gauge changes. Soak a Kimwipe in isopropyl alcohol and rub each bearing's face for a second or two, in small circles. Keep the alcohol off the platform's panels: it damages them. Switch the pucks off.
3. **Check the LEDs.** Each platform's LED driver connects to the LEDs and, by USB, to the platform's computer for power. These are normally left connected; if the LEDs don't light, check them first.
4. On the Hardware tab, the platform should show **Ready**. Run a **thruster check** to hear each valve open in turn.

Never set a platform down on the table without floating it, and never let a platform slide off the table: the bearings are easily damaged.

## After an experiment: taking a platform off the table

1. Put the platform's stand where it will go, the right way round, so it won't crush the bearings or the tubing.
2. Float the platform: **Pucks** on, for **one platform at a time**.
3. Grip it firmly from below, lift it evenly, and carry it to the stand; a second person helps set it down.
4. Release the air: unscrew the pin that feeds the thrusters and the pucks (counter-clockwise, seen from the gauges) until both gauges read zero.
5. Switch the pucks off on the Hardware tab, then power the platform down. The air tank can now be unscrewed for refilling. If it is hard to remove, the system isn't fully depressurised yet.

## At the end of the day

1. **Cover the table.** Lower the barriers first (the cover won't go on otherwise) and put the bolts back in the *Table* drawer. Lay the folded cover in the middle of the table, unfold one end, then the other, and straighten the corners. No need to clean the table.
2. **Charge the platforms** (all three can charge at once). For each:
   1. Make sure it is switched off.
   2. **Unplug the barrel jacks from the platform's computers** (they must not be on the battery while it charges: it can break them).
   3. Plug the charger, on the timed wall adapter, into the platform's power panel, and press the adapter's button. The charger's light turns red, and green when charged. The adapter switches off at 5 AM.
3. **Put the alcohol away** in the yellow cabinet. Chemicals left out can bring a fine.
