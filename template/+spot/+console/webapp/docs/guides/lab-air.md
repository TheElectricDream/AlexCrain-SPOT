# The Air Compressor

The platforms' tanks are filled from the lab's compressor.

> [!CAUTION]
> Compressed air at these pressures can cause serious injury. Wear **ear defenders and safety glasses** while the compressor runs: a line rupture is loud enough to damage hearing permanently. **Never disconnect a tank while the system is pressurised.**

The filling line has four points where air can escape: valves **A**, **B** and **C**, and **D**, where the tank connects.

## Filling an empty tank

1. Connect the tank at D. It stays connected until the whole system is depressurised (step 11).
2. Make sure valve A is open, and open valve B.
3. Put on ear defenders and safety glasses.
4. Switch the compressor on, with A and B open.
5. When the compressor is up to speed and air vents from B (about 5 s), close B.
6. After about a minute, you hear air flowing through A: close A.
7. Wait about 7 minutes while the tank fills; the compressor stops by itself when it is full.
8. Purge moisture: open B for one second, then close it.
9. Open C for one second, then close it.
10. Open A and let all the air drain.
11. When the compressor's gauge and the gauge at A both read zero, the tank can be disconnected. Leave A open, and B and C closed.

## If the compressor fails

If it is safe, switch the compressor off. If it is not safe, leave the area and call Carleton's Campus Safety Services, (613) 520-3612.
